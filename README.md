# Emily in wonderland

A short, playful fairytale puzzle adventure about one person being the hero, the princess, and every role in between.

## The story

A storybook kingdom has mixed up its roles: a hero is supposed to rescue a princess, but Emily is both of them. To find a new ending, she switches between four sides of herself and uses the one that fits each moment. The roles are facets of the same character, not separate people.

The tone is warm and lightly comic. The game celebrates Emily's different strengths without treating any part of her as a problem to fix.

## Rooms and puzzles

| Room | Role | Puzzle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clover Gate | Jester | Get a very serious gatekeeper laughing so the gate opens. |
| Moon Library | Listener | Hear the quiet clue that leads to a missing page and hidden door. |
| Windy Courtyard | Hero | Move the fallen stones and clear the path to the tower. |
| Star Tower | Princess | Open the storybook mirror and step out of the tower yourself. |

Each room has one role-specific puzzle. Choosing another role near the puzzle gives a hint instead of blocking progress permanently. Solve a room's puzzle, then use the glowing doorway on the right to continue.

## How to play

1. Open `emily-almost-rescue.html` in a modern desktop or mobile browser.
2. Move Emily around the room and approach the highlighted puzzle.
3. Switch to the matching role, then interact.
4. Read the dialogue, solve each room, and reach the mirror in Star Tower.

No installation, server, or internet connection is required.

### Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Arrow keys or WASD | On-screen directional buttons |
| Interact / advance dialogue | E, Space, or Enter | Interact button |
| Switch role | 1 Hero · 2 Princess · 3 Jester · 4 Listener | Select a role button |
| Pause or resume | Escape | — |
| Restart | Restart button | Restart button |

The Sound button toggles simple, locally generated sound effects.

## Included features

- Four short rooms with role-based puzzle logic and room transitions
- Four selectable roles, each with a distinct ability and hint
- Dialogue that advances with the keyboard or on-screen button
- Feedback when the selected role does not fit a puzzle
- Title, pause, ending, and restart screens
- Keyboard controls and touch-friendly movement
- Responsive layout for desktop and mobile
- Browser-local progress saving
- Original scene art drawn with Canvas; no external images or libraries

## Progress and privacy

Progress is saved in the browser's local storage on the device where the game is played. It does not sync between browsers or devices. Restarting begins a fresh run; finishing the story clears the saved progress.

## Playtest checklist

- Start a new game and advance the opening dialogue.
- Move with both the keyboard and the touch controls.
- Try the wrong role near a puzzle and confirm a useful hint appears.
- Solve each room with its intended role and enter each next room.
- Complete the mirror scene and reach the ending.
- Test restart, Escape pause/resume, role switching, Sound, and saved progress.

## Prototype limitations

- This is a compact four-room prototype rather than a long game.
- Character and environment art are simple original Canvas drawings, not finished illustrations.
- Sound effects are basic synthesized tones.
- Progress is stored only in the current browser's local storage.
